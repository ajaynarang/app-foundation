# Members and roles

A workspace is the shared space your team works in. One person can belong to several workspaces and switch between
them; each browser tab stays in the workspace it opened.

## Roles

Every member has one role in each workspace they belong to.

- **Owner**: created with the workspace. Manages billing, the AI keys and every setting, and cannot be removed.
- **Admin**: manages members and most settings.
- **Member**: uses the product day to day.

A person can be an owner in one workspace and a member in another.

## Inviting teammates

Owners and admins invite people from Settings > Members. The invitee receives an email link; following it adds them
to the workspace with the role chosen in the invitation. Pending invitations are listed under Settings > Invitations,
where they can be resent or revoked.

## Removing someone

Removing a member ends their access to that workspace at once. Their past activity stays in the history.
