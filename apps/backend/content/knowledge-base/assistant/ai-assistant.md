# The AI assistant

The assistant answers questions about the product and can act in your workspace through its tools.

## Asking questions

Open the assistant and type a question in your own words. When an answer draws on the knowledge base, the documents
it used appear under the answer as sources.

## Actions that change data

The assistant asks before it changes anything. It shows a confirmation card describing exactly what it will do, and
nothing happens until you confirm. Cancelling leaves your data as it was.

## Conversation history

Past conversations are listed in the history panel, newest first. You can remove one you no longer need. Each person
keeps up to 30 active conversations; starting a new one past that archives the oldest.

## When the assistant cannot answer

If the workspace has reached its AI spending cap, or no AI provider is configured, the assistant says so instead of
answering. A workspace owner can connect the workspace's own AI key under Settings > AI Keys; usage on that key is
billed to the workspace by its provider and does not count toward the cap.
