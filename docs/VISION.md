# Product vision

> **Binding doc** (see "Binding docs" in the root `CLAUDE.md`). Empty in the starter: fill it first, in the
> product's first days, before any domain code. Each section says how to fill it; delete that line once it is
> filled. Change it later only as a deliberate amendment in the pull request that changes direction.

## Vision statement

_How to fill this: one or two sentences. Who it is for, what it lets them do, and why that matters. A reader should
be able to say no to a feature by reading only this._

## The problem

_How to fill this: the problem in the customer's words, today's workaround, and what that workaround costs them._

## Who it's for

_How to fill this: the first customer and the first user, as concrete people (role, company size, what a normal day
looks like). Name who it is not for. The personas used for testing are written from this section._

## The core model

_How to fill this: the three to six ideas the product is built on, one paragraph each. These become the entities in
`docs/domain-map.md`._

## Product principles

_How to fill this: the rules that settle a design argument ("a human signs, the AI drafts"; "nothing is deleted, only
archived"). Each one short, each one testable._

## What we will not do

_How to fill this: the non-goals. A feature request that lands here is answered by pointing at this list._

## Success looks like

_How to fill this: what is true in three months and in a year, as observable facts (a customer does X without help;
Y happens in under a minute)._

## Open questions

_How to fill this: questions that are tracked but do not block building. Each gets an owner and the date it was
raised; when one is answered, move the answer into the section it changes._
